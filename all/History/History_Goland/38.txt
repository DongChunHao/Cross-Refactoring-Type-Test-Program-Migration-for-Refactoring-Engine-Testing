Due to Go operator precedence, `-` and `+` have equal precedence and evaluate left-to-right, changing the original meaning (`a - (b + c)` becomes `(a - b) + c`). The same bug applies to other left-associative binary operators like multiplication and division.
Expected behavior: Generate `a - (b + c)` to retain the original semantics.

}

// Expected correct output after inline variable
